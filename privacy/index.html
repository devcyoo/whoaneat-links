Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Website visitors

whoaneat.com doesn't use accounts, forms, ads or analytics cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, which process standard request data (such as IP address) to deliver and protect the site. When you click a product link you go to Amazon, whose own privacy policy applies; Amazon may use cookies to credit our affiliate account.

WhoaNeat Publisher (TikTok integration)

WhoaNeat Publisher is used only by WhoaNeat to upload its own videos to its own TikTok account. With the account owner's permission it uses TikTok's APIs to read basic profile information and to upload or publish videos. Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's computer, are never shared or sold, and are used only for that account. We don't collect data from other TikTok users. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings.
